## Top 5 Issues

---

### 1. Customers are building your replacement — CRITICAL

61% of revenue comes from 4 companies (Microsoft ~19%, Meta ~11%, Amazon ~6%, Google ~6%).
All four are simultaneously funding AMD, custom chips, and in-house alternatives.

**So what?**
→ If 1–2 customers cut orders by 30% → direct revenue loss ~12%
→ Sentiment breaks → multiple compresses from 23x to 14x
→ Combined effect: **-40–55% stock price without any technology failure**

This alone justifies a PASS.

---

### 2. CapEx digestion cycle is coming — CRITICAL

Hyperscalers spent $600B+ on AI infrastructure in 2026.
AI services revenue from that infrastructure: $25B — an 18:1 investment-to-revenue ratio.
Goldman Sachs base case: capex growth slows in 2027.

**So what?**
→ Revenue growth slows from 65% → ~15–20%
→ Multiple compresses as the growth story cracks
→ Earnings miss AND multiple compress at the same time → **-30–45% stock**

The trigger is not a crash — it is one quarter of flat capex guidance.

---

### 3. China is a political call option, not revenue — CRITICAL

China was 13% of NVIDIA revenue in FY2025 ($17B).
The H20 ban materialized overnight → $4.5B write-off, zero DC revenue in Q1 FY2027.
The Trump H200 scheme: NVIDIA keeps only 75 cents of every China dollar. Zero orders placed in 60+ days.

**So what?**
→ $25–50B/year of potential China revenue is gone, possibly permanently
→ Can close again with zero warning, as proven
→ Model China as **$5–8B/year probability-weighted option**, not recurring demand

---

### 4. CUDA moat eroding exactly where it matters — HIGH

NVIDIA dominates AI model training (~95% share). But training is only ~20% of AI compute.
Inference — 75–80% of AI compute and growing — is where CUDA is losing ground.
AMD's MI355X benchmarks within single digits of NVIDIA B200. Meta deployed 6GW AMD. AWS Trainium: $1/hour vs $3/hour for NVIDIA.

**So what?**
→ 15 percentage points of inference share lost by FY2028 = **$25–35B/year foregone revenue**
→ This doesn't show in today's financials — it will appear in FY2028–2029
→ By the time it's visible, it's too late to exit without pain

---

### 5. $95.2B in non-cancellable supply commitments — HIGH

NVIDIA committed to buying $95.2B of components from TSMC — 6x more than one year ago.
The H20 write-off proved what happens when a policy changes overnight and inventory is stranded.

**So what?**
→ A 20% demand softening event = **$15–25B impairment** (vs $4.5B last time)
→ Last time a $4.5B write-off erased **$600B of market cap in one session**
→ A 3–5x larger impairment would be proportionally worse
