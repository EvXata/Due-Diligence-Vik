## Key Competitors (with approximate market shares)

**Data Center GPU/AI accelerators:**
- NVIDIA: ~85% share of merchant AI accelerator GPUs
- AMD (MI300X / MI325X / MI350): ~9% share, growing on hyperscaler diversification
- Intel (Gaudi 3): ~2% share, behind on roadmap
- Cloud-internal silicon (AWS Trainium/Inferentia, Google TPU, Meta MTIA, Microsoft Maia): collectively ~4% but doubling YoY

**Networking (post-Mellanox):**
- NVIDIA Spectrum-X / Quantum InfiniBand: dominant in AI fabric
- Broadcom Tomahawk: leading merchant Ethernet, growing share at hyperscalers
- Arista, Marvell: secondary players

**Gaming:**
- NVIDIA GeForce: ~80% discrete GPU share
- AMD Radeon: ~17%
- Intel Arc: ~3%

## Regulatory / Litigation Posture (active)

- **US export controls (China):** BIS rules updated Oct 2023 + Dec 2024 + Apr 2025 progressively tightening; H20 (China-spec) revenue ~$4–5B/quarter is the at-risk wedge. Total China DC exposure ~12–15% of DC revenue.
- **EU AI Act:** General-purpose AI obligations effective Aug 2026; compliance burden modest, indirect customer impact via cloud providers.
- **FTC inquiry (2024–25):** preliminary review of CUDA lock-in / bundling practices with networking; no formal complaint as of May 2026.
- **France competition authority:** ongoing review of AI chip pricing practices, opened June 2024; no charges yet.
- **Antitrust litigation:** Patent suits from Polaris, Xockets, and several others — all in early stages, not material to thesis.

## Known Bear Arguments (carry into hypothesis-tester)

1. **Customer concentration in 4 hyperscalers:** ~50% of DC revenue from Microsoft, Meta, Google, Amazon combined. If any pulls capex, immediate revenue impact.
2. **Custom silicon migration:** Google TPU v6/v7, AWS Trainium2, Meta MTIA v2 all reportedly competitive on inference; could compress NVIDIA share from 85% → 70% by FY28.
3. **China export controls:** $15–20B annual revenue at risk if H20 / B40-China variants banned.
4. **AI capex digestion risk:** Hyperscaler capex grew from $200B (2023) to projected $560B (2025). If AI revenue from end-customers (enterprise) does not catch up, capex must normalize → demand cliff late 2026 or 2027.
5. **Gross margin compression:** 75% gross margins assumed stable but pricing power may erode as AMD MI350 + customer-internal silicon gain credibility.
6. **Valuation:** NTM P/E ~32x at current price vs S&P 500 ~22x; PEG ~0.9 — supported only if growth stays >40% through FY28.

**Short interest:** ~1.2% of float as of May 2026 (low — not a contrarian short).
