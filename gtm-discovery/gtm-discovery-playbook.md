## 2. Discovery Questions

Ask only questions that classify the product vector:

1. What is the decision deadline?
2. What is the entry price / valuation / ticket size?
3. Are you looking for permission to buy, or reasons not to buy?
4. Who must be convinced: analyst, PM, partner, IC, board, seller, lender?
5. What would make you walk away immediately?
6. What would make you proceed despite a bad base case?
7. Do you need a 10-sec page, a 5-min pre-meeting brief, or an IC-grade master?

Routing:
- "Need reasons not to buy" -> Bear Case Generator.
- "Still want to buy if protected" -> Acquirer Protection Package.
- "Need to defend dissent in IC" -> Deep Audit.
- "Need quick view before call" -> DD Short Fast.
- "Have a strong bull thesis already" -> Bull/Bear Pair.
